Illustrative e-commerce checkout project notes. These statements are sample input and do not assert legal or regulatory obligations.

The storefront shall show the current product price, available quantity, and delivery estimate before a customer places an order.
The checkout shall send payment details directly to the configured payment provider and shall not retain raw card credentials.
The order service shall assign a unique order reference after the payment provider confirms the transaction.
If payment confirmation is delayed, the storefront shall show a pending state and prevent duplicate order submission while status is checked.
Customers shall be able to view order status and request cancellation before the order enters fulfilment.
The operations team shall be able to review an audit history of order status changes and manual refunds.
